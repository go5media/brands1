About UK Safety Camera Network

UK Safety Camera Network Ltd provides CCTV installation, repairs and maintenance for homes and businesses across London and Surrey. Its commercial work helps fund its community safety camera network.

This equipment section is written from USCN’s perspective as a CCTV business and network operator. It is part of USCN, rather than a separate independent review organisation.

What a brand listing means

These pages bring together information about the brands and equipment we use, alongside our reasons for choosing them. Inclusion does not establish a commercial partnership, manufacturer certification or approval.

Brand information explains our equipment choices. Individual reviews describe our experience with particular products and the applications they may suit.

How to read our reviews

We separate practical observations from manufacturer specifications. A feature appearing in a menu is not the same as a feature whose performance we have evaluated.

Practical results are reported in the context of the test conditions. Observations from a particular installation or test should not be treated as guaranteed performance in every setting.

We identify untested areas and avoid assigning a numerical score without a defined assessment. Results depend on the lens, focus, lighting, exposure, settings, scene and subject movement.

Sources and changes

Manufacturer sources are linked in the relevant article. Specifications, compatibility and support dates can change; check current manufacturer information before purchasing.

Further testing should be added to the relevant review with an updated date and a clear description of what changed.
